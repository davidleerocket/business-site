ThinkFastSolutions Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

ThinkFast Solutions respects your privacy and understands that resumes and career information may contain personal details. We collect and use only the information reasonably necessary to evaluate your request, provide services, communicate with you, and process payment.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your resume and other career materials
  • Your LinkedIn profile or professional links
  • Your employment history, education, skills, career goals, and target roles
  • Messages and other information submitted through our intake form or email
  • Basic payment and transaction information

Please do not submit Social Security numbers, financial account details, passwords, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information.

How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Review your request and career materials
  • Prepare and deliver the services you purchase
  • Communicate with you about your request
  • Process and document payments
  • Respond to questions or support requests
  • Maintain necessary business and transaction records

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Confidentiality

Your resume, career history, goals, and related materials are treated as confidential. We will not publish or share your materials with other customers, contact your employers, or use your name, documents, results, or testimonial for promotional purposes without your permission.

Payments and Service Providers

Payments are processed securely through Stripe. ThinkFast Solutions does not directly store your full payment card information.

We may also use established third-party services, including Google Forms, email and cloud-storage providers, GitHub Pages, and document-editing tools. These providers may process limited information as necessary to perform their services and operate under their own privacy policies.

Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable precautions to protect client information and limit access to submitted materials. However, no online service, email system, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, respond to follow-up questions, maintain required business records, or resolve disputes.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information and career materials you have submitted. Some transaction or business records may need to be retained for accounting, legal, or fraud-prevention purposes.
