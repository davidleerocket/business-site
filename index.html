About

Hi, I'm Isaac, founder of ThinkFast Solutions.

I learned firsthand that a strong resume can be the difference between getting interviews and getting ignored.

During my final semester of college, I thought I had my career figured out. I was working for a company I expected to join after graduation, but as graduation approached, I realized it wasn't the right fit. I left with nothing lined up and just weeks to find a new opportunity in an incredibly competitive job market.

After plenty of rejection emails, I stopped treating my job search like a guessing game. Instead, I built a repeatable system for tailoring resumes, applying strategically, and improving with every application. That process led to interviews with multiple companies and ultimately helped me launch my career at a major financial institution.

Today, I use that same approach to help other job seekers strengthen their resumes, apply more strategically, and land more interviews.
